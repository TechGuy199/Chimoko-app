An assistant that everyone with a login can ask, by typing or by voice: it looks things up in the app (sales, stock, customers, accounts...), explains how to do things, and can prepare a sale, a pre-order, a payment or a delivery change, which happens only when the person presses Confirm. It sees only what the person asking could open themselves: an operator never gets accounts, staff pay or supplier details.

It is run by Anthropic's Claude, so what it looks up (customer names and phone numbers, sales, and for an admin the accounts) is sent to Anthropic to answer. Voice uses the browser's own speech: what is said is usually turned into words by the browser maker's online service (Google for Chrome, Microsoft for Edge, Apple for Safari), and speech recognition is not available in Firefox.
