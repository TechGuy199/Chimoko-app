Assistant

{% if user.demo_expires_at %}

The assistant is switched on for this demo company, with a small allowance: {{ assistant_info.limits.business_questions }} questions today.

{% else %}

An assistant that everyone with a login can ask, by typing, by voice or with a photo: it looks things up in the app (sales, stock, customers, accounts...), explains how to do things, says what needs attention today and plans plantings. It can prepare sales, pre-orders, payments, delivery changes, purchases (from a photo of a supplier's receipt), plantings with their trays (from a photo of a tray tally), tray payslips, stock transfers, supplier payments and money entries, each of which happens only when the person presses Confirm, and it writes reminders for customers that the person sends themselves. It says what to restock and what to watch out for, gives everyone an evening brief, and remembers the preferences people tell it. It sees only what the person asking could open themselves: an operator never gets accounts, staff pay or supplier details.

It is run by Google's Gemini, so what it looks up (customer names and phone numbers, sales, and for an admin the accounts), and any photo sent to it, is sent to Google to answer. Use a Gemini key from a Google project with billing switched on: on Google's paid terms your records are not used to improve Google's products, but on its free terms they can be.

Wake and stop phrases

{{ csrf_field() }}

For everyone in the business. With Listen on (the ear in the assistant's panel, in Chrome, Edge or Android), the wake phrase and a question ("Hey Mark, what is low?") ask it out loud, and it answers in the voice each person chose; hands-free mode needs no wake phrase. While listening is on, the browser sends what the microphone hears to its own speech service (Google's, in Chrome) to pick out the phrases.
