Statutory contribution rules

Each contribution has two separate rules. The employee rate is deducted from the employee's pay. The employer rate is paid by the business on top of the salary and never reduces the employee's pay. Enter 0 where a side pays nothing.

ContributionEmployee rate (%)deducted from payEmployer rate (%)paid by the businessTotal remitted

Where these rules apply

  • Every payslip made with Compute Payslip.
  • Every payslip brought in through Import → Salaries & Wages, wherever the file leaves a contribution blank.
  • Each payslip stores the four amounts worked out at the time, so changing a rate here affects new payslips only. Payslips already saved keep their figures.
