Statutory contribution rules

Each contribution has two separate rules. The employee rate is deducted from the employee's pay. The employer rate is paid by the business on top of the salary and never reduces the employee's pay. Enter 0 where a side pays nothing.

Planting trays

Used by Planting Records. A planting is entered as a number of trays; the seeds planted are the trays multiplied by the holes in each tray. A casual worker is paid for the trays they planted instead of for days worked.

Kinds of tray

If you plant in more than one kind of tray, list them here with what a casual worker is paid for planting one of each. On Planting Records every entry then says which kind was planted, and each person is paid by kind: 2 Big trays at 0.75 and 1 Small tray at 0.25 is 1.75. With no kinds listed, every tray is paid at the one rate above.
