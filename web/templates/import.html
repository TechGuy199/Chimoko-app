Purchases

Each row is one purchase. It is added to inventory and to the Procurements log, exactly like the "Log a Purchase" form.

  • Required: Item Code, Quantity, Unit Cost. Name is required when the item code is new.
  • Optional: Category, Department, Selling Price, Supplier, Invoice No, Purchase Date, Batch No, Date Planted, Expiry Date, Payment Status, Amount Paid.
  • Payment Status is Paid (default), Unpaid or Partial Payment. Partial Payment needs an Amount Paid.
  • An item code you already have adds the quantity to that item's stock.
  • A blank Selling Price keeps the item's current price (a new item uses the unit cost).
  • Departments that do not exist yet are created.
  • Importing the same file twice adds the stock twice.

Sales

Each row is one item sold. Rows with the same Invoice No become one invoice; rows with no Invoice No each get their own, numbered automatically.

  • Required: Quantity, plus an Item Name or an Item Code you already have.
  • Optional: Invoice No, Date, Customer, Phone, Unit Price, Payment Status, Amount Paid.
  • A blank Unit Price uses the item's POS price. A blank Date uses today.
  • Payment Status is Paid (default), Unpaid or Partial Payment. Partial Payment needs an Amount Paid.
  • For a multi-row invoice, the customer, date and payment details are read from its first row.
  • An Invoice No that already exists is rejected, which protects against importing a file twice.

Salaries & Wages

Each row is one payslip for one employee for one month. Imported payslips appear under HR & Payroll, in the monthly summary, and can be printed.

  • Required: Employee Name, Pay Month, Basic Salary.
  • Optional: NRC Number, Employment Type, Expected Days, Worked Days, Bonuses, Deductions, Employee NAPSA, Employee NHIMA, Employer NAPSA, Employer NHIMA, Net Pay.
  • Name each bonus or deduction with its own column, headed like Bonus: Transport or Deduction: Advance. Add as many as you need.
  • Employees are matched by NRC Number when the file has one, otherwise by name. Anyone not yet registered is registered with the salary in the file.
  • Blank Expected Days are counted from the pay month and your HR settings; blank Worked Days mean a full month.
  • Blank NAPSA and NHIMA cells, for the employee and for the employer, are worked out from the statutory rules in HR Settings. Amounts you provide are kept as they are.
  • Blank Net Pay is calculated. A Net Pay you provide is stored as given, even if it differs from the calculation.
  • Pay Month can be 2026-10, October 2026 or Oct-26.
  • An employee who already has a payslip for that month is rejected, which protects against importing a file twice.
