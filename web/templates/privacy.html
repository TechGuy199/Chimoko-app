Privacy Policy

Effective {{ legal.effective }}. Applies to {{ legal.app }} at {{ legal.site }}.

{{ legal.app }} is a point-of-sale and business records app run by {{ legal.operator }} ("we", "us"). A business registers an account and records its sales, purchases, stock, plantings, customers, suppliers, staff and pay in it. This policy says what information the app holds, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible for what

Each business that registers decides what it records about its own customers, suppliers and employees, and is responsible for having the right to record it. We hold and process that information on the business's behalf, only to run the app for it. Information about the people who sign in (their usernames and settings) is held by us to provide the service.

What we hold

  • Accounts: the business's name and details, usernames, a one-way hash of each password (never the password itself), roles and permissions, and each person's display preferences (theme, typeface, text size).
  • Business records: everything a business enters, such as invoices, payments, customers' names and phone numbers, suppliers, inventory, plantings, customer requests, employees (including names, NRC numbers, pay and absences), payslips and activity logs.
  • Alerts and logs: in-app alerts, a record of who did what and when, and the phone number set for WhatsApp alerts.
  • Assistant (when a business switches it on): the questions asked, short notes the assistant has been asked to remember (shown and deletable in Settings), and a usage log (counts, not content).
  • Google Drive connection (when an admin connects one): the Google account's email address, an access token and refresh token (kept encrypted), the id of the backup folder the app created, and the ids of the backup files it saved.

Cookies and storage on your device

The app uses a sign-in cookie to keep you signed in and protect forms, and a cookie that remembers your display preferences. It also keeps some data in your browser so pages open quickly and parts of the app work offline, and remembers small choices such as whether the assistant speaks. We use no advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics services.

Google user data

When an admin chooses Connect Google Drive on the Backups page, Google asks them to allow the app two things: to know the email address of the Google account, and the drive.file permission. That permission lets the app create, see and change only the files and folders it made itself. It cannot see, read or delete anything else in the Drive.

  • We use this access only to save the business's own backup files (Excel workbooks and CSV files of its records) into a folder named after the business, to replace them with newer backups, and, if the admin turns it on, to do so automatically each night.
  • The email address is shown on the Backups page so the admin knows which Drive is connected.
  • We do not read other files, do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train any AI or machine-learning model, and do not share it with anyone except as needed to provide this backup feature, to comply with the law, or with the admin's consent. People at {{ legal.operator }} do not read it, except when the admin asks us to help with a problem, for security reasons, or where the law requires.
  • The tokens are stored encrypted. An admin can disconnect at any time on the Backups page, which withdraws the app's access at Google and deletes the tokens from our database; access can also be removed from the Google account's security settings. Backups already saved stay in the Drive, belong to the Drive's owner, and can be deleted there.

{{ legal.app }}'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. To run the app we use these service providers, who process information only to provide their service:

  • Vercel hosts the app, and its database service stores the records.
  • Google (Gemini API), only when a business switches the assistant on: questions, spoken questions (audio), and the records the assistant looks up to answer them are sent to Google to produce the answer and, if talking back is switched on, the spoken reply. The app asks Google not to store these requests.
  • Google Drive, only when an admin connects it, as described above.
  • WhatsApp messaging providers (Meta's WhatsApp Business service or CallMeBot), only when alerts by WhatsApp are set up: the alert text is sent to the phone number the business chose.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google to load them.
  • Some browsers send speech to their maker to turn it into text when you use the assistant's microphone.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the app, its users or others from fraud or harm.

How long we keep it

Records are kept for as long as the business's account exists, so the business can rely on its books. A 24-hour demo business, its sample data and its login are deleted automatically when the demo ends. When a business's account is deleted, its records and logins are deleted from the live database; copies in our hosting provider's backups expire in the normal course. Backups a business saved to its own computer or Google Drive are under its control.

Security

The app is served only over HTTPS, passwords are stored as hashes, Google tokens are encrypted, every business's records are kept apart from every other business's, and staff accounts see only what their permissions allow. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it to yourself.

Your choices and rights

You can see and correct most information in the app itself, and an admin can export all of the business's records from the Backups page. Depending on where you live (for example under Zambia's Data Protection Act, 2021), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to how it is used. If you are a customer, supplier or employee of a business that uses {{ legal.app }}, please contact that business first, as it controls those records; we will help it respond.

Children

The app is for businesses and is not meant for children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell admins in the app of any important change before it takes effect.
